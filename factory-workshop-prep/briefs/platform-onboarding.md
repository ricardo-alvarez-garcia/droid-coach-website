# Discovery brief: Harbor Systems (fictional)

SE: Sam Patel · Call date: 2026-11-19

## Who we spoke to
The platform engineering lead. The lead and three engineers hired this quarter would attend.

## What they maintain
A platform repository with deployment tooling and shared services. New hires say it takes weeks to feel comfortable in it.

## Current problem
New hires struggle to find their way around the repository and cannot yet explain how they would validate a first change before opening a pull request.

## What they want from the workshop
New hires should be able to orient themselves in an unfamiliar repository and explain how to validate a first change. The lead wants a workflow that future hires can repeat without the lead in the room.
